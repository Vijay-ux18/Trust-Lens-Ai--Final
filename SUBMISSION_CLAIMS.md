# TrustLens AI — Submission Claims

## Approved claims
- TrustLens performs non-executing/static file analysis.
- PE files are routed through the dedicated PE model; other supported formats use the multi-format path.
- TrustLens achieved **99.48% accuracy on the independent PE holdout validation set** (41,415 samples).
- The documented 99.8% multi-format result is a synthetic proof-of-concept result.
- A raw-file validation pipeline is included for independent labelled real-file testing.

## Do not claim
- Do not call 99.8% universal real-world accuracy.
- Do not call the 99.48% PE result universal accuracy or overall cross-format accuracy.
- Do not describe the 99.80% synthetic multi-format result as real-world accuracy.
- Do not report a raw-file accuracy percentage until the exact labelled raw files have actually been analysed and the generated evidence is retained.

## Recommended wording
> TrustLens AI achieved 99.48% accuracy on an independent 41,415-sample PE holdout validation set. The 99.80% multi-format result is a synthetic proof-of-concept result. These figures apply only to their respective evaluated corpora and are not universal accuracy claims.
