The dataset is not bundled with TrustLens; users should obtain it from an authorized source and follow the source citation/redistribution terms. ## CIC-Evasive-PDFMal2022 benchmark

TrustLens includes a derived PDF benchmark model trained from the CIC-Evasive-PDFMal2022 labelled feature table. The source CSV is not redistributed with this project. Any use of the benchmark must retain the original CIC/UNB citation and terms. The benchmark result is reported as a PDF-specific external validation result, not universal multi-format accuracy.
